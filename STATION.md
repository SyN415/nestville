# Nestville v7 — Railroad Station

Drag your creature into the station. They board, ride the train across the map, and get off at the **same stop**.

## Safe vs lethal
- Station building + platform = safe
- Riding = safe (passenger)
- Raw rails during a passing train = still game over

## Flow
1. Drag onto the platform / door, or walk up and press E
2. Boarding hop into the passenger car
3. Train crosses the whole map (camera follows)
4. It turns around and comes back
5. They hop down on the same platform

## Rewards
Happy +, a few coins, a little energy spent.

## Must not
- Dump them onto the tracks at the end
- Flatten a passenger
- Start a ride if they are already dead or too tired
