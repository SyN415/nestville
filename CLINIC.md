# Nestville v9 — Clinic and colds

Nothing in Nestville can end a creature. No game over. No crush.

## Cold
Once in a while a pet gets a sniffle. They walk a little slower and look droopy. They do not get worse into an ending.

## Doctor
Bring them to the Clinic (walk + E, or drag to the door).

Checkup animation:
1. Waiting bench
2. Stethoscope
3. Say ahh
4. Pink medicine + a sticker

Then take 3 doses from the dock **Medicine** button until they are well.

If you wait it out, the cold fades on its own after a few minutes. Still no ending.
