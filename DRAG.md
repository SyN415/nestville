# Nestville v4 — Drag your creature

Grab the character with the pointer and pull them through town.

- Pointer down on the body: pick up (lift pose, they follow the cursor)
- Pointer up: drop. If you drop on a building, you can enter it.
- Pointer down on empty ground: they walk there on their own gait.
- WASD still works and cancels a drag.
- Sketched characters are draggable too.
