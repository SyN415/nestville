# Nestville

A Tamagotchi-style creature raising game. Every creature is unique — even two turtles don't walk the same.

**Open `index.html` in a browser.** No build step.

## Play
- Pick a creature (turtle, duck, Bounce, Mochi, or Ember the fox)
- Watch them arrive: eggs hatch, mammals get a cozy den welcome, Bounce inflates from a marble
- Name them, then walk Nestville
- Feed, bathe, sleep, cuddle, and visit city spots

## Controls
- **WASD / arrows** or click the ground to walk
- **E** or tap a building prompt to enter
- Bottom dock: Feed · Play · Bathe · Sleep · Profile

## Creatures
| Character | Origin | Walk |
|---|---|---|
| Pip the Turtle | Egg | Slow shell-rock |
| Puddles the Duck | Egg | Waddle + tail bob |
| Bounce | Inflates from an orb | Squash-hop roll |
| Mochi | Cozy den arrival | Soft jiggle toddle |
| Ember the Fox | Cozy den arrival | Light diagonal trot |

Saves automatically to `localStorage` (`nestville_save_v1`).
