# Nestville v6 — Railroad

An east-west rail line crosses the city. Trains come often. Being on the tracks when one passes is game over.

## Fairness
- 8 seconds after load or birth: no train
- Every train is telegraphed: bells, blinking lights, gates down for 3.5s
- Then the train passes (~2.8s)
- Cartoon flatten only. No gore.

## Hit rule
If the creature is on the rail band while the train occupies that X, they are hit.
Drag, walk, click-to-walk, fetch, and standing all count.

## Crossings
Plaza (~1120) and Park (~1860). Gates block those gaps during warn + pass.
Open track between crossings is still dangerous — that is the accidental-drag case.

## Game over
- Flatten pose, dust, train keeps going
- Card: "The train came through. {name} was still on the tracks."
- Save marked dead. Continue is hidden.
- Only path forward: make someone new.
